The short answer: your eSIM's phone number is not sent by email. It lives inside the eSIM, and you will find it in your phone's settings once the eSIM is installed.

  • iPhone: Settings → Mobile Service (or Cellular) → the eSIM line → My Number.
  • Android: Settings → About phone → SIM status → Phone number. The wording varies by manufacturer.
  • If the field is empty, restart, wait for signal, or call another phone from the eSIM line and read the number off its screen.

You bought an eSIM with a phone number, got an email with a QR code, installed it, and now you want the number so you can send it to your hotel, your host or your family. It is the first thing almost every customer asks, and the answer is simple: it is in your phone's settings. This guide shows exactly where on iPhone and on the most common Android phones, and what to do if it does not show up.

Why the number is not in the email

The number is assigned to the eSIM when it is activated and is stored on the eSIM itself. The email you receive after purchase contains the QR code and instructions, and tells you where to find the number once it is installed. So there is no point searching the email or the QR code for it. Install first, then check your settings.

Finding the number on iPhone

The main route

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Mobile Service. Depending on your iOS version and region, this may be called Cellular or Mobile Data.
  3. Under "SIMs", tap the new eSIM line. If you have not named it, it may be called "Secondary", "Travel" or the provider's name.
  4. Your number is shown on the My Number row.

Tip: give the line a label

On the same screen, tap "Mobile Plan Label" (or "Cellular Plan Label") and rename it "Travel" or "Abroad". It makes the line easy to tell apart from your home line when you call, text or choose which line uses data.

Choosing which line you call from

In the Phone app, the keypad shows the line that will be used at the top. Tap it to switch to the eSIM. Under Settings → Mobile Service → Default Voice Line you can set the eSIM as the default for calls while you travel.

Finding the number on Android

Every manufacturer words it slightly differently, but you are always looking for "SIM status" or the SIM manager.

PhoneWhere to look
Samsung GalaxySettings → About phone → Status information → SIM card status → My phone number. Also: Settings → Connections → SIM manager
Google PixelSettings → Network & internet → SIMs → choose the eSIM (the number is near the bottom). Or Settings → About phone → Phone number
Xiaomi / Redmi / POCOSettings → About phone → All specs → Status → SIM status
OnePlus, OPPO, Motorola and othersSettings → About phone → Status → SIM status, or search "SIM" in the settings search bar

The quickest route on any Android phone: open Settings and type "phone number" or "SIM" into the search bar.

Empty field or "Unknown"? Try this

  1. Check the eSIM is installed and switched on. In your mobile settings, the line's toggle should be on.
  2. Wait for signal. Sometimes the number only appears once the line has connected to a network. If you are somewhere the plan does not work yet (for example, the French number before you reach Europe), it may only show up after you land.
  3. Restart the phone. It fixes a surprising number of cases.
  4. Call another phone from the eSIM line, such as a travel companion's. Your number appears on their screen. This is also the surest way to check the number before you send it to anyone.
  5. Still stuck? Contact us with your order number and we will look into it.

Do not delete the eSIM to "reinstall" it. The QR code works once. After deletion you cannot simply scan it again, and you may lose the number.

What your account shows

In your account on our site, the eSIM page shows the plan, when it started and when it ends. If you installed later than the purchase date, you can set the installation date there and the end date updates. The number itself is in your phone's settings, as above. Your account is also where you renew a US or global number before the plan ends.

Once you have the number

  • Save it to your contacts under your own name, say "Me abroad", so you can copy and share it quickly.
  • Send it in advance to your hotel, host, rental company and family, with the country code (+1 or +33).
  • Check the end date in your account. US and global numbers can be renewed before the end to keep the number, and we email you a reminder 48 hours before.

Frequently asked questions

Will the number arrive by email?

No. The number is stored on the eSIM and appears in your phone's settings after installation. The email contains the QR code and instructions.

Can I find out the number before installing?

No. The number is assigned when the eSIM is activated, and you see it after installation.

The number shows without a country code. What is it?

For the US and global numbers the country code is +1. For the French Europe number it is +33. When you give the number to someone abroad, include the code.

How do I call from the eSIM rather than my home line?

On iPhone, tap the line name at the top of the keypad and choose the eSIM. On Android, the phone usually asks which SIM to use, or you can set a default in the SIM settings.

I deleted the eSIM by mistake. Is the number gone?

Contact us straight away with your order number and we will check with the provider what can be done. We cannot promise the eSIM can be restored.
