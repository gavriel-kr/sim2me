Why a global number works well for Israel

It covers the whole region

Israel trips rarely stay inside one country. It is a US number

For American visitors this is the big one. Your family, office and friends in the US reach you on a +1 number, which is a domestic call for them. You can give it to your airline, your travel insurer and your tour operator, and it works the same in Israel as it does at home.

It lasts as long as your stay

Plans run from a week to a year. A semester abroad, a volunteer program or a long sabbatical can sit on one number from arrival to departure, and a renewal before the end keeps it for longer.

If most of your calls will be with Israelis, a local Israeli SIM with a +972 number may suit you better. The global number shines when your calls are with people back home, or when you move between countries.

Setting it up before you fly

  1. Check your phone supports eSIM and is carrier-unlocked. Phones bought on a US carrier contract are often locked; ask your carrier to unlock it before the trip. See the compatible devices list.
  2. Buy the plan and you will receive the eSIM by email straight away.
  3. Install it shortly before you fly. On plans with a number, validity starts on the day you install, so do not install weeks in advance.
  4. Find your number in Settings → Mobile Service (or Cellular) → the new line → My Number on iPhone, or Settings → About phone → SIM status on Android. Our guide to finding your eSIM number has more detail.
  5. On landing, turn on Data Roaming for the eSIM line and choose it for mobile data. Keep roaming off on your home line to avoid charges from your carrier.

Does the global number work in Israel?

Yes. Israel is on the list of 168 countries, and the eSIM connects to Israeli mobile networks.

No. It is a US number with the +1 code. People in Israel who call it are calling a US number.

Will it work if I visit Petra or Sinai?

Yes. Jordan and Egypt are both on the list, so the same number keeps working when you cross the border. Allow a few minutes for the phone to connect to the new network.

No. The French number works in 36 European countries, and Israel is not one of them. For a number that works in Israel, choose the global number.

Can I keep the number after my trip?

Yes, as long as you renew before the plan ends. Renewals go onto the same eSIM and keep the same number.
