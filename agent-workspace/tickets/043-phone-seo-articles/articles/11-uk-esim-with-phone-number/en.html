When to choose the French number

For most UK trips the French number gives you more for less: 20GB that lasts a month and unlimited calls to your hotel, restaurants and drivers. If you carry on by Eurostar to Paris or Brussels, or fly to Amsterdam or Dublin, the same number keeps working. In the UK it runs on Orange's partner networks.

When to choose the global number

  • You want to keep the number for future trips, for example if you travel to London for work several times a year. The global number can be renewed; the French one cannot.
  • Your route includes places outside the European list, such as Jersey, Guernsey, the Isle of Man or Gibraltar, or you continue to the US.
  • You want a US number that friends or clients in the States can call at domestic rates.

In the UK the global number connects to EE and Three (per the provider's network list at the time of writing).

Four UK trips and what suits each

A long weekend in London

Museums, a West End show and some shopping. If nobody needs to call you, a data-only UK eSIM costs less (about $4.80 for 5GB for a month at the time of writing). If you have bookings that are arranged by phone, the French number gives you a number and data for $33.90.

A family road trip in Scotland

Highland driving, B&Bs in small villages and a rental company that may need to reach you. The French number for each adult. Some rural and mountain areas have weak signal on every network, so download offline maps.

Frequent business trips to London

The global number on a long plan (180 or 365 days), so British clients know one number for you, trip after trip.

Starting university in the UK

For the first month, before you open a UK contract, the French number gives you a European number and plenty of data. After 30 days it ends and cannot be renewed, so plan your switch to a local line.

Phone tips for the UK

  • Rental firms and pick-up services sometimes call before collection. Give them your number with the full country code (+33 or +1).
  • On the London Underground many central stations have signal, but tunnels between stations often do not.
  • Install on the day you fly or the day before. Validity starts on the day you install.
  • For long calls home, use a calling app over data. The French number's unlimited calls are within Europe.

Before you buy: your phone must support eSIM and be carrier-unlocked. There are no refunds after purchase.

Frequently asked questions

Does the French number really work in the UK after Brexit?

Yes. The UK is one of the French number's 36 countries, alongside Switzerland and Norway, which are also outside the EU.

Are calls to UK numbers really unlimited?

Yes. Calls within the European coverage area are unlimited, UK numbers included. Calls to other countries have a 30-minute allowance.

Can I get a UK number?

Not from us. If you need a UK (+44) number, buy a SIM from a British network when you arrive.

What about Ireland?

Ireland is covered by both the French number and the global number.

Which number works in Jersey?

The global number. The Channel Islands, the Isle of Man and Gibraltar are not on the French number's list.
