Three things to know before you buy

1. It cannot be renewed

After 30 days the plan and the number end. You can buy another plan if you stay longer, but it comes with a new number. If keeping the same number matters to you, choose the global number, which can be renewed.

2. Validity starts at installation

The 30 days count from the day you install, not the day you land. Install the day before you fly or on the day itself, over Wi-Fi.

3. International calls are limited to 30 minutes

Unlimited calling covers the European coverage area. Calls to other countries have a 30-minute allowance. For long calls home, use WhatsApp or FaceTime over the plan's 20GB.

Installing and using it

  1. Check your phone supports eSIM and is unlocked.
  2. Buy the plan and receive the eSIM by email.
  3. Install it on the day you fly or the day before. See the installation guide.
  4. Find your French number in your settings. On iPhone: Settings → Mobile Service (or Cellular) → the new line → My Number. More in how to find your eSIM number.
  5. When you land, turn on Data Roaming for the eSIM line and set it as your data line. Leave roaming off on your home line.

Before you buy: there are no refunds after purchase, because the eSIM is delivered immediately. A phone locked to one carrier will not accept the eSIM.

Frequently asked questions

Does the Europe number work in the UK?

Yes. The United Kingdom is one of the 36 countries, as are Ireland, Switzerland and Norway.

Does it work in Turkey?

No. Turkey is not covered by the French number. The global number does cover Turkey.

What happens after 30 days?

The plan and the number end. You can buy a new plan, but it comes with a new French number.

Are calls to my home country unlimited?

No. Unlimited calls are within the European coverage area. Calls to other countries have a 30-minute allowance.

Why do some sites show up in French?

The data connection exits through France, so some sites guess you are in France. Switch the language on the site; the connection itself works normally.
