Phone tips for Thailand

  • Install close to departure. Validity starts on the day you install.
  • Save your number with the +1 code and send it to your guesthouse, tour agency and family.
  • Between islands there are stretches at sea with no signal. That is normal; the line reconnects when you reach land.
  • Download offline maps for the north and the smaller islands.
  • For long calls home, use a calling app over data and keep your minutes for local calls.

Before you buy: your phone must support eSIM and be carrier-unlocked. There are no refunds after purchase.
