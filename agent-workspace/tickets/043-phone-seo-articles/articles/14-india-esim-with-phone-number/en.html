Phone tips for India

  • In the mountains (Himachal, Ladakh, Uttarakhand) coverage is patchy on every network, and remote valleys have none. Arrange things in advance and check whether your guesthouse has Wi-Fi.
  • On trains and overnight buses there are long stretches without signal.
  • Give your number with the +1 code. For local businesses it is an international call, so many will prefer WhatsApp.
  • Install close to departure. Validity starts on the day you install.

Before you buy: your phone must support eSIM and be carrier-unlocked. There are no refunds after purchase.
