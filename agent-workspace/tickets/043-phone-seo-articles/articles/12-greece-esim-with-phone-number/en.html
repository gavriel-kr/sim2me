Why a phone number is so useful in Greece

Ferries

Ferry companies usually ask for a mobile number when you book so they can tell you about changes. In summer, when strong winds disrupt schedules, a working number helps you find out quickly if a sailing is delayed or cancelled.

Car, quad and boat hire

On Rhodes, Crete, Naxos and Zakynthos, the small rental firms work by phone. If something happens on the road, you need to reach them and they need to reach you.

Tavernas

Popular seafront restaurants and those in picture-postcard villages take sunset bookings by phone, and some call back to confirm.

Holiday lets

Island hosts arrange arrival times, keys and parking by phone. A European number makes it easy for them to call you.

Families

When the teenagers head to a different beach or into town, calls between your numbers are unlimited, just as at home.

Combining Greece with Turkey, Albania or Cyprus

  • Cyprus: covered by both the French and the global number.
  • Turkey: day trips to the Turkish coast leave from Kos, Samos and Rhodes. Turkey is not covered by the French number, but it is covered by the global number.
  • Albania: boats run from Corfu to Saranda. Again, only the global number works there.
  • Italy: a Greece and Italy trip stays on the French number.

If your trip includes a day in Turkey or Albania, you can take the global number for the whole trip, or take the French number for Greece and rely on data and messaging apps for the day across the water.

Phone tips for Greece

  • Install on the day you fly or the day before. The 30 days count from installation.
  • On ferries there are stretches of open sea with no signal. That is normal.
  • In Crete's mountain villages and on small islands signal can be weak. A regular call needs less signal than data does.
  • For long calls home, use a calling app over the 20GB. The unlimited calls are within Europe.

Before you buy: your phone must support eSIM and be carrier-unlocked. There are no refunds after purchase.
